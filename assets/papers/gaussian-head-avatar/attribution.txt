Independent ImageGen explanatory illustration for this archive. Fictional fully clothed adult portrait and independently depicted geometric/feature representations;not source-paper artwork or actual experimental predictions.
Method facts: Yuelang Xu et al., Gaussian Head Avatar: Ultra High-fidelity Head Avatar via Dynamic Gaussians, arXiv:2312.03029v2, Sections3-4; https://arxiv.org/html/2312.03029v2#S4 . This is the original Gaussian Head Avatar,not the later HHAvatar dynamic-hair extension.
Training uses synchronized multi-view RGB video and cameras for each person. BFM fitting provides expression,pose and3D landmarks. SDF and DMTet guidance yield neutral geometry;mesh vertices/features initialize neutral Gaussian positions/features,and learned deformation/color MLPs transfer to the Gaussian stage.
At runtime,a learned avatar receives expression coefficients,head pose and a render camera. Separate expression/pose MLPs and landmark-distance weights affect Gaussian position,color,rotation,scale and opacity. Neither FLAME binding nor LBS drives this method. Neutral Gaussian features do not imply stored neutral RGB colors.
Dynamic Gaussians render512x512x32 image features,then super-resolution generates2048x2048 RGB. Feature tiles are independently illustrated2D learned channels,not depth/normal/voxel outputs. The conceptual render illustrates an image output,not a measured mesh-accuracy result. Actual avatar preparation remains person-specific training,not instant single-photo reconstruction.
