Independent ImageGen explanatory illustration for this archive. One generation,no edits. Fictional fully clothed adults and living room;conceptual representations,not source-paper artwork or actual model predictions/experiments.
Method facts: Minhyuk Hwang et al.,4D Human-Scene Reconstruction from Low-Overlap Captures (StudioRecon),arXiv:2607.09125v1,Section3 and AppendixD-E; https://arxiv.org/html/2607.09125v1#S3 .
Synchronized fixed-camera RGB inputs are separated into static background and dynamic-human branches. Person masks and first-frame camera/depth estimates support preparation. Generated first-timestep camera views supplement background supervision;human regions are excluded. Rendered background ellipsoids and sparse surface samples schematically illustrate Gaussians,not verified geometry of unseen room regions.
Cross-view identity association,keypoint triangulation,scale fitting and SMPL initialize people. Grey mesh displays are opaque neutral priors for initialization/driving,not final clothing meshes. People are optimized on original videos over time,while background optimization uses supplemental views. SMPL/LBS drives human deformation;the temporal MLP predicts color and opacity residuals,not clothing3D geometry displacements.
The desired camera trajectory renders composed background/person Gaussians. Recursive diffusion enhancement uses reference images,optical flow and previously enhanced outputs for consistency. Mild artifacts and their cleanup in the illustrative before/after views are hypothetical,not benchmark observations,measured gains or proof of improved3D geometry. No realtime,feed-forward-only,training-time,FPS,metric accuracy or universal image-quality claim is introduced.
