Inputs are synchronized4-6view2K RGB with RVM foreground masks,not asynchronous video or sensor-depth/GT-mesh inputs. Camera calibration may be supplied optionally;in that case camera prediction/BA are skipped. The illustrated unposed path estimates cameras and refines camera poses only using4LM steps. Runtime network weights remain fixed;each frame is independently reconstructed.
Low-resolution DINO and high-resolution CNN features support sparse foreground and shared scene tokens. Predicted depth and Gaussian attributes produce per-view pixel-aligned1K parameter maps. Position/scale/rotation colors are independent conceptual channel visualizations,not actual encoded values,normal maps or UV texture atlases. Opacity is distinct from depth. Source supervision uses a normalized first-camera coordinate frame and scale;the graphic promises no metric depth accuracy.
H265 video codecs transmit Gaussian maps;the receiver decodes them and instantiates3D Gaussian primitives for interactive viewpoint rendering. The output is not a solid mesh,body template,rigged export,room reconstruction or claimed holographic display. No FPS,bandwidth,network latency or benchmark-performance values are invented. Model inference time is explicitly distinguished from capture/transmission/display latency. No temporal consistency guarantee is added.
